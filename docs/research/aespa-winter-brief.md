# Research brief: Winter (aespa) fansite

Verified against Wikipedia, SM press coverage (Soompi, Billboard, Korea JoongAng Daily), PAPER, iF Design, and fan wikis as of 2026-10-05. Where a fact comes only from fan wikis, it is flagged.

## 1. aespa and the SMCU / Kwangya lore

- Debut: November 17, 2020 with "Black Mamba" under SM Entertainment. Members: Karina (leader), Giselle, Winter, Ningning. Name = "Avatar X Experience" + "aspect": "meeting another self and experiencing a new world" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aespa).
- Fandom: MY, lore meaning "the most precious friend" in KWANGYA; SM also framed it as AI + Y(ou).
- Lore glossary (SM Culture Universe, SMCU):
  - **æ / ae (æ-Karina, æ-Giselle, æ-Winter, æ-Ningning)**: each member's digital counterpart with its own free will, generated from the member's data. Styled with the ligature "æ".
  - **SYNK**: the state of connection between a human and her æ; also the name of every aespa tour (SYNK: Hyper Line 2023, Parallel Line 2024-25, aeXIS Line 2025-26, Complaexity 2026-27).
  - **FLAT**: the æ's native virtual world, where the members first meet their æ.
  - **KWANGYA ("wilderness")**: a boundless, ruleless, infinite space between dimensions; the wilderness beyond the FLAT where Black Mamba lives.
  - **Black Mamba**: the villain, a shape-shifting digital entity that corrupts the SYNK and severs human and æ.
  - **nævis**: the guide / gatekeeper AI who opens the way into KWANGYA. SM debuted her as a standalone virtual artist on September 10, 2024 with "Done".
  - **REKALL**: the process by which the æ leave the FLAT and enter the real world.
  - **P.O.S (Port of Soul)**: the portal; æ can cross only when it opens. The Savage album case is shaped like it.
- Narrative arc: Black Mamba (connection severed) -> Next Level (journey to KWANGYA) -> Savage (confronting Black Mamba, nævis joins) -> Girls (fight at full scale). From MY WORLD (2023) onward SM de-emphasized explicit lore.

## 3. Winter

- **Name**: Kim Min-jeong. Born January 1, 2001, Busan. Position: vocalist, dancer. First member revealed. Scouted 2016, trained four years. Also in SM supergroup Got the Beat (2022).
- **Lore role**: "armamenter", the one skilled in weaponry (PAPER). æ-Winter in the Black Mamba era had long silver-white hair and pale blue-white styling.
- **Fan-wiki profile** (Kprofiles, fan-sourced): representative emoji star, representative color ivory, animal Siberian Husky, number 44, nickname "baby rabbit"; other fan nicknames "gyeoulie", "mindeungie". Left-cheek dimple, drinks a lot of milk.
- **Persona**: PAPER: "steely smolders, sharp movements and blunt edges" on stage, quiet introvert off stage, "delicate but also deadly"; voice that can "croon and growl", "piercing falsetto" and "honey tone".
- **Iconic moments**: Got the Beat "Step Back" fancam 10M views in 25 days; electric guitar solo opening SYNK: Hyper Line (2023); long sleek dark hair at debut, platinum/honey blonde and pastel pink/lavender later; pneumothorax surgery April 2024 and return.
- **Solo work**: "Once Again" w/ Ningning (Our Blues OST, 2022); "Floral Sense" (Yesung feat. Winter, 2023-02-27); "Win for You" w/ Yim Si-wan (2023-09); "Voyage" (Castaway Diva OST, 2023-11-19); "Nobody" w/ Soyeon and Liz (2023-11-16); "With You" (My Demon OST, 2023-12-08); "Officially Cool" w/ Bang Ye-dam (2024-04-02); "The Moment I First Saw You" (Teenieping, 2024-07-03); "Spark" (co-written, Synk: Parallel Line, 2024-10-09); "Hunjung Yeonsuh" (The Tale of Lady Ok OST, 2024-12-01); "On Such a Day" (Resident Playbook OST, 2025-04-19); "BLUE" (co-written pop-rock, 2025-11-17); "Speed of Summer" (alt punk-rock single with Dingo Music, August 2026).
- 2025-26: Red Cross and UNICEF donations; UNICEF Honors Club (March 2026); KM Chart Best Popular Solo Female (2026); ambassador for Polo Ralph Lauren, New Balance, AHC. No full solo album as of October 2026.

## 4. aespa visual language

- Recurring motifs: glitch/datamosh transitions; liquid chrome and metal lettering; holographic/iridescent fabrics; Y2K cyber; hyperreal CGI avatars; portal/wormhole (P.O.S) imagery; "SYNK" HUD-style overlays; the "æ" ligature as brand mark; "future warrior" fashion with LED, transparent materials, geometric forms; neon vs. metallic contrast representing light/dark and real/virtual.
- Typography: no public aespa typeface; custom angular, condensed, techno display lettering, paired with a neutral sans. Free stand-ins: Exo 2 or Saira (condensed, heavy) for display; Orbitron, Rajdhani, Saira Condensed for titles; Inter or Work Sans for body. Each era re-skins the display face (chrome for Savage, matte metal for Armageddon, stark mono for Whiplash). SM also mixes elegant serifs (Drama, Rich Man credits) against the techno face.

## Design takeaways

1. Base palette: near-black #0B0B10, chrome gradient #C9CED6 to #6E7480, ice-blue accent #A9D6FF (Winter's æ), violet #6A3FC9 and acid green #B6FF3B as lore accents. Keep neon as 5 to 10 percent of surface.
2. Era theming as a token set: each era page swaps accent tokens (Drama #B3121B red/black; Supernova #8EFF5A on #1A0F2E; Whiplash pure #000/#FFF; Dirty Work rust #8C4A2F and steel; Rich Man ice #DCE9F2 and amber #E0A33A; Lemonade #F7E85A, mint #A8F0D1, pink #F6A9D8).
3. Type pairing: Exo 2 or Saira Condensed Black (uppercase, tight tracking) for headlines, Inter for body, plus an editorial serif (Cormorant or Playfair) for quotes and era titles. Render "æ" ligature as a brand glyph.
4. Chrome text: CSS gradient fill + subtle inner shadow, animated sheen on hover.
5. Glitch micro-motion: RGB-split and horizontal slice on page transitions and hero image hover; clamp to 200 to 300 ms and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
6. "SYNK" HUD: thin 1px cyan rules, monospace timestamps, corner brackets on cards, a "SYNK rate" style progress bar for scroll.
7. Portal/wormhole: radial concentric gradient as the hero background. Use it as a loading state.
8. Winter identity layer: star glyph, ivory #F3EFE6 surfaces for her personal sections, husky and snowflake icons sparingly; "armamenter" as a motif for sharp blade-like edges and dividers.
9. Timeline component: 2020 to 2026 era strip with color swatch per era, tap to reveal MV, concept photo, and Winter's hair color that era.
10. Solo discography module: card per song with OST drama poster, date, co-writing badge for Spark, BLUE, Speed of Summer.
11. Dark mode default; light mode uses ivory background.
12. Avoid fabricated lore: only use terms in section 1; label fan-sourced facts as fan-sourced in UI copy.
