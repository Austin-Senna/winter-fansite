## B. Member profiles

Positions below are the ones stated on Wikipedia; fan-wiki "main/lead" labels (kprofiles) are noted separately. Lore role titles come from SM's 2021 short film "æspa ep1. Black Mamba - SM Culture Universe" as reported by Good Morning America (https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/culture/story/what-to-know-about-aespa-the-latest-kpop-girl-group-taking-the-world-by-storm-86215881); the power descriptions attached to them circulate on fan sites and TV Tropes (https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Music/Aespa) and should be flagged as fan-sourced. Symbols/colors/animals are from kprofiles (https://kprofiles.com/aespa-profile/), fan-sourced; the official group color is "Aurora" (purple-blue gradient).

### Karina (Yu Ji-min, 유지민)
- Born 2000-04-11, Suwon, Gyeonggi-do (kprofiles adds raised in Bundang, Seongnam). Leader; dancer, rapper, vocalist (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karina_(South_Korean_singer)). kprofiles: Leader, Main Dancer, Lead Rapper, Sub-Vocalist, Visual, Center.
- SMCU role: **Rocket Puncher**. Fan-described powers: superhuman strength and speed via a robotic arm, eye glows red when active (TV Tropes; KWANGYA 101 https://17caratkpop.substack.com/p/kwangya-101). In the Black Mamba MV, æ-Karina is the avatar the Black Mamba corrupts, cutting the SYNK (Wikipedia Black Mamba; envimedia https://envimedia.co/kwangya-korner-learn-about-kpops-first-metaverse-group-aespa/).
- Fan-sourced symbol/color/animal: heart, blue, whale (kprofiles).
- Solo: "Up" (SYNK : PARALLEL LINE, 2024-10-09), her first solo top-10 on Circle Digital and first solo music-show win (Show! Music Core); "GOOD STUFF" (SYNK : aeXIS LINE, 2025-11-17, hip-hop/R&B, co-written); "16 Bit" (SYNK : COMPLæXITY, 2026-09-14); feature on Jannabi's "May the Tenderness Be with You!" (2025-04-28); "Serenade" with Winter (2026-08-09). Also Got the Beat member (2022).
- Fan-referenced moments: opening theme stage at 2024 MAMA Osaka with Yeonjun, Rei, Yoshi, Sung Han-bin (Soompi https://www.soompi.com/article/1703606wpp/...); Nike global ambassador, first Korean celebrity in that role (July 2025, Wikipedia); May 2025 "red jacket / No. 2" Instagram post deleted and apologized for during the presidential election (Wikipedia); Netflix *Agents of Mystery* cast.

## C. Storyline per album

Framework: æ are avatars "made up of digital information" from the members' data; SYNK is the link between member and æ; FLAT is the æ's resting dimension; KWANGYA is the digital wilderness ruled by the Black Mamba; nævis is the AI gatekeeper; REKALL is time spent knowing one's æ; P.O.S is the portal through which KWANGYA entities reach the real world (KWANGYA 101; envimedia Kwangya Korner). Season 1 = Black Mamba through Girls. From MY WORLD on, SM kept a nominal "Season 2" label but stopped shipping explicit lore in the music; concepts became theme-driven. This is marked per era.

**Black Mamba (2020-11-17).** Introduces the worldview: members meet their æ through a mobile app called SYNK; the Black Mamba, "an enormous black snake", interferes with the connection and threatens their world, and the MV ends with a glitching distorted figure (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Mamba_(song)). The lore beat is the corruption of æ-Karina, which severs SYNK (envimedia Kwangya Korner). Story carrier: "Black Mamba". SM later released the 10-minute film "ep1. Black Mamba" assigning each member's role (GMA).

**Next Level (2021-05-17).** SM called it a sequel to Black Mamba "during which the connection to their ae was cut off"; the MV shows the members "venturing out to wilderness to find Black Mamba" (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Level_(Aespa_song)). Fan readings describe it as a battle cry toward KWANGYA, with the glitching æ-human transformation and Karina's "only one survives" vision (KWANGYA 101). It is a remake of A$ton Wyld's Hobbs & Shaw track; Lee Soo-man directed performance and camera. Story carrier: "Next Level".

**Savage (2021-10-05, EP).** SM's description: aespa proceeds to KWANGYA "with the help of nævis" while "narrating the group's ongoing efforts to defy the Black Mamba"; physical versions were titled P.O.S, Synk Dive and Hallucination Quest, naming the lore devices (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savage_(Aespa_EP)). Press coverage: the EP tells of aespa entering KWANGYA, where they face Black Mamba-induced hallucinations and nævis makes sacrifices (envimedia; Guidon https://vantage.theguidon.com/aespas-savage-pulls-you-deeper-into-kwangya/). Story carriers: "Savage" and "aenergy"; "Lucid Dream" and "I'll Make You Cry" are mood pieces.

**Girls (2022-07-08, EP).** The title track's lyrics "express aespa and ae-aespa's continuation of their journey together with naevis", and the MV stages the battle against the Black Mamba that "wrap[s] up aespa's SM Culture Universe Season 1 story" (Bandwagon https://bandwagon.asia/articles/aespa-release-second-mini-album-girls-music-video-sm-entertainment-2022-smcu-listen; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girls_(Aespa_EP)). "Illusion" (pre-release) and English single "Life's Too Short" are not lore tracks. Fan readings note the CCTV observer who dissolves into pixels (KWANGYA 101). Story carrier: "Girls".

**MY WORLD (2023-05-08, EP). Lore drop point.** SM framed it as Season 2 with the members "traveling to the real world from Kwangya"; Karina: "We were like warriors in the virtual world, but in this real world, we look just like our peers" (Korea Times https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/amp/entertainment/k-pop/20230509/welcome-to-my-world-aespa-invites-people-to-real-world; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_World_(Aespa_EP)). Pre-release "Welcome To MY World (feat. nævis)" invites nævis into the real world, after which "an anomaly continues to occur" (Wikipedia). Concept instead: high-teen confidence; "Spicy" is a 10/10 teenager anthem; NME noted nævis references became "easily missed". Winter said the SM leadership change "had no direct influence". Only "Welcome To MY World" carries story.

**Drama (2023-11-10, EP). Nominal lore only.** SM's teaser text: the members are "breaking out from the trauma caused by a series of events with Season 1's SYNK OUT and Hallucination Quest, and also the unknown anomalies in Season 2", writing stories "in their own way" (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drama_(Aespa_EP); Korea Times https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/www/art/2023/10/398_360886.html). Concept instead: a hip-hop dance track whose lyric is "every story begins with you"; Complex called it "a song about choosing to be the problem". No MV lore beats beyond the trauma framing. Story carrier: "Drama" (loosely).

**Armageddon / Supernova (2024-05-13 / 2024-05-27, album). Season 2 label, abstract.** SM: the album carries "the narrative of the second season of aespa's worldview that expands into the multiverse, beyond the real and digital worlds", under the slogan "I define myself" (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armageddon_(Aespa_album); KOCCA https://welcon.kocca.kr/en/support/content-news/4576). "Supernova" uses the stellar explosion as "an event that opens a door to another dimension", "heralding the start of season two"; "Armageddon" positions aespa as the predator/potential villain (Complex). Concept instead: cosmic self-definition; no named lore entities appear in the singles. Story carriers: "Supernova", "Armageddon".

**Whiplash (2024-10-21, EP). No lore.** SM's description: an EDM track with fast bass and house beats about "moving forward without hesitation with your own standards and criteria"; the Wikipedia article records no SMCU references (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whiplash_(Aespa_EP)). Concept instead: fashion-forward "double take" iconography (Complex). Preceded by the four solos on SYNK : PARALLEL LINE (2024-10-09). No story carrier.

**Dirty Work (2025-06-27, single album). No lore.** Teaser melted jewelry into the title logo; MV shot at a Hyundai Steel mill in Dangjin; performance video shot on iPhone 16 Pro with Apple; Karina pitched it as continuing aespa's "metallic" sound (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirty_Work_(Aespa_song); Korea JoongAng Daily https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/entertainment/aespa-to-release-new-single-dirty-work-on-june-27/11987969). Concept instead: "good-girl-gone-bad" industrial glamour (Complex). Versions: Korean, feat. Flo Milli, English, instrumental.

**Rich Man (2025-09-05, EP). No lore.** Karina: "a dance song with a catchy, rough guitar sound, conveying the message of self-confidence and self-love" (Dork https://readdork.com/track/aespa-aespa-on-rich-man). The MV trailer "I am a Rich Man" was directed by Lee Ok-seop with actor Koo Kyo-hwan; "rich" is framed as inner strength and independence, not money (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rich_Man_(EP); Spotify Newsroom MY VAULT campaign https://newsroom.spotify.com/tag/aespa). Concept carrier: "Rich Man"; "To The Girls" closes as a message to fans.

**WDA / LEMONADE (2026-05-11 / 2026-05-29, album). Partial lore return, visual only.** SM says WDA captures "the growth of aespa" within an expanding worldview; the MV depicts a world where digital and physical boundaries are blurred, "entities resembling aespa yet distinctly separate" create conflict, and the group breaks through a fracture named "Complaexity" to reaffirm their existence (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WDA_(Whole_Different_Animal)). "Complæxity" then names the 2026-27 tour (Bandwagon https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/stepping-into-a-new-era-aespa-announces-2nd-album-lemonade-2026-27-aespa-live-tour-synk-____-____). "LEMONADE" itself is a "when life gives you lemons" EDM track with "a positive message of turning any trial and hardship into opportunity" (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lemonade_(Aespa_album)). Story carrier: "WDA" (MV); LEMONADE is concept-only.

**Japan releases (context).** Hot Mess (2024-07-03) is a saxophone-led dance track with "a message of independence", pop-punk styling, with an anime-avatar MV teaser (Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Mess_(Aespa_song); Leisurebyte). ATTITUDE (2026-03-06) is the *Kill Blue* anime theme about nothing stopping you being "me" (Bandwagon https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/aespa-exude-attitude-for-kill-blue-anime-theme-song-listen). KISS N TELL (2026-07-24) swaps the futuristic image for pink, sweet styling over a house-rooted dance-pop lead (Complex https://www.complex.com/music/a/alex-ocho/aespa-kiss-n-tell-japanese-mini-album). None carry SMCU lore.
